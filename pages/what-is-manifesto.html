How to Upload Content on Manifesto

A short guide to submitting pages, images, and videos to Manifesto.

Written by the Manifesto Founder

How can I upload a page?

You can submit a page in one of the following formats:

HTML file

You can submit an HTML file with inline CSS and no JavaScript. Images, videos, and custom fonts can be included in a ZIP archive together with your HTML file.

HTML submissions will be reviewed manually before publication.

TXT or Markdown file

You can also submit a plain-text file (.txt) or a Markdown file (.md). These formats are suitable for articles, guides, and other text-based content.

How to submit

  1. Prepare your file in a supported format.
  2. Put the necessary assets in a ZIP archive if needed.
  3. Send your submission through our Telegram bot.
  4. Wait for our team to review it.

Submit a page via Telegram Bot

How can I upload an image or video?

Simply send your image or video through our Telegram Bot, the name you want to put in the author, a short description, and a title. No additional files or preparation are needed.

  1. Open our Telegram Bot.
  2. Send the image or video you want to share with the details required.
  3. Wait for verification and approval.

Submission rules

  • Only submit content you have permission to share.
  • Respect other people's privacy and rights.
  • HTML submissions must not contain JavaScript.
  • Make sure your files follow the required formats.
  • Do not submit sexual content, pure gore, contents meant to hurt someone just to publish it online.
  • Submissions may be rejected after review.
  • The moderators HAVE the right to decide to remove a content EVEN after is approved, this is for safety reason only, and an explenation WIL be given.

Submitting content does not guarantee publication. All submissions are subject to review.
